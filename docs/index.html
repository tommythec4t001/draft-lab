Draft Lab is a free Windows app for League of Legends. Follow professional Fearless Draft series game by game, see which champions are already locked out for the rest of the series, and get clear draft help in your own champion select.

Download for Windows free Source code on GitHub

Fearless Draft, live

In Fearless Draft, a champion picked in one game can't be picked again for the rest of the series, by either team. Every game changes the next one. Draft Lab makes that layer visible while the series is being played.

  • Live series board. CBLOL, LCK, LEC, LPL, LTA, Worlds, MSI and more, with the series score updating game by game.
  • Locked champions. Every champion already used in the series, grouped by the team that picked it.
  • What's still available. The champions free for the next game, in a tier list ranked by their competitive priority this season.
  • Draft reading. Lane-by-lane advantages, a draft rating for both sides with the reasons behind it, and a team composition breakdown.

Help in your own champion select

When your champion select starts, Draft Lab opens with it and reads the draft through the League Client. It suggests options and explains why. You make every decision.

Suggestions

One clear list per team

Picks and bans ranked for the current draft, each with short reasons: lane counters by champion class, synergies with your team and Solo/Duo win rates with enough games behind them.

Your team

Composition at a glance

Physical and magic damage split, frontline, engage, crowd control, power curve, and warnings like "the enemy has dive and nobody protects your carries".

One click

Import runes and items

Review the rune page, summoner spells and item set first, then import them into the client. Draft Lab uses one page of its own and never deletes your pages without asking.

Builds ranked by what actually wins

An item's win rate is biased: players who are already ahead buy different items. Draft Lab ranks items, runes and summoner spells by Win Probability Added.

WPA = win rate − win probability just before the choice
  • Items and boots: the win probability before each purchase comes from the game minute and the team gold difference at that moment.
  • Runes, spells and starting items: from the lane matchup, right after the draft.
  • Also on every champion page: matchups (good and bad against), high-elo match history, leaderboards for Solo/Duo and Flex, and player lookup by Riot ID.

How Draft Lab uses Riot data

Statistics are aggregated: no individual player is profiled. Requests are cached and rate limited. With a production key, the key stays on Draft Lab's own server and never ships inside the app.

Fair play and privacy

  • No in-game advantages. Draft Lab works before the game: champion select and statistics. It doesn't show information during a match that the game itself doesn't show.
  • Enemies stay anonymous. Only the champions picked by the enemy team are used, never who they are.
  • You decide. Suggestions always offer several options with reasons. Nothing is picked, banned or imported without a click from you.
  • Free. No betting, no gambling, no ads in the game or in the client.
  • Your data stays with you. Settings and history live on your computer. Draft Lab has no accounts and sells no data.

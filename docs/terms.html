2. What Draft Lab is

Draft Lab is a free Windows app for League of Legends players and fans. It offers:

  • Fearless Draft Live: follows professional series as they are played, showing which champions are locked out under Fearless rules and which remain available, ranked by competitive priority.
  • Champion select help: pick and ban suggestions with the reasons behind them.
  • Builds and statistics: runes, items, matchups and win rates aggregated from ranked Solo/Duo matches, with items and runes ranked by Win Probability Added.
  • Leaderboards and player lookup by exact Riot ID.
  • Optional client tools: importing a rune page, item set and summoner spells, and a "+FPS" graphics preset.

Features may change, be paused or be removed at any time, including when Riot Games' policies require it.

4. The League client

When the League client is open on your computer, Draft Lab reads information that the client already shows you, such as your champion select. Draft Lab only changes something in the client when you click a button to do it:

  • Rune pages: Draft Lab uses one rune page of its own, called "Runas Draft Lab". It shows you the runes before importing them, and if all your pages are in use it asks which one to replace. It never deletes a page without asking.
  • Item sets and summoner spells: imported only when you click, and only during champion select for spells.
  • "+FPS" preset: lowers graphics settings through the client's own settings, saves your previous settings first and lets you restore them with one click. It also switches Windows to the High performance power plan and asks Windows to run the game on your faster graphics card; both can be changed back in Windows settings. It doesn't work while a match is in progress.

Back up anything you care about before using these tools. You are responsible for the changes you choose to make.

5. Fair play

Draft Lab is designed to keep the game fair:

  • It doesn't show any information during a match.
  • It never identifies players on the enemy team. Only the champions they pick are used.
  • It never picks, bans or plays for you, and its suggestions always offer several options.
  • It has no betting, gambling or ads in the game or the client.
